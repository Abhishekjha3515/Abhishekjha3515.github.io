Adaptive Temperature in Contrastive Learning on Imbalanced Data

Abhishek Kumar · Advisor: Prof. Debashis Sen · Co-Advisor: Prof. Saumik Bhattacharya (M.Tech Thesis, 2023–2024)

Investigated representation collapse and tail-class gradient suppression in instance-based contrastive learning (SimCLR) on long-tailed benchmarks (CIFAR-10-LT, CIFAR-100-LT). When data exhibits heavy class imbalance, high-frequency head classes dominate the negative contrastive gradient field on the unit hypersphere $\mathbb{S}^{d-1}$, suppressing minority tail representations. Investigated and applied the Model-Aware Contrastive Learning (MACL) adaptive temperature strategy to imbalanced datasets, dynamically adapting temperature based on representation alignment $\tau_a = \tau_0 \cdot [1 + \alpha (A - A_0)]$ to mitigate head-class gradient dominance. Achieved a verified >4 percentage-point gain in linear-probing accuracy over baseline SimCLR, reaching peak accuracies of 73.36% on CIFAR-10-LT and 36.91% on CIFAR-100-LT under high imbalance ($\beta = 0.1$). Contributed the MACL loss module upstream to the official Lightly PyTorch SSL repository (lightly-ai/lightly), merged into production releases.
